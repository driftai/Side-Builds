Paste URLs and search terms together. URLs identify themselves normally; for several search terms on the same line, wrap each term in square brackets such as [Attack On Titan] [Bleach] [Dragon Ball]. A lone unwrapped phrase such as Code Geass still runs as one normal browser search.

URLs may be separated by spaces or new lines. Bulk search terms use [square brackets]. Completed spaces are compared automatically for identity, provenance, corroboration and disagreements. Ctrl/Cmd + Enter analyzes the batch.
