Piano Auto Player v0.6.18 - SPARSE TIMING CONTINUITY

Piano Auto Player v0.6.16 - RELATIVE PACING CONTINUITY

- Fixes local timing jumps caused by independently correcting adjacent attack clusters by different amounts.
- Dense passages now use a slew-limited timing-correction curve, so a delayed attack cannot make the immediately following key arrive prematurely relative to it.
- The allowed correction change scales with the actual inter-onset gap; very fast passages receive the strongest continuity protection.
- Genuine phrase gaps reset the correction naturally instead of dragging timing bias across rests.
- Keeps acoustic onset sensing as evidence, but prevents its per-cluster corrections from distorting local rhythm.
- Preserves v0.6.15 target-key alias handling, v0.6.14 acoustic reattack arbitration, v0.6.13 release-safe note-offs, and source-space pitch fusion.
- Adds diagnostics for relative timing jumps softened, phrase timing resets, and raw/stabilized correction-step size.
- No new setup or model download is required.

Piano Auto Player v0.6.15 - TARGET-KEY ALIAS LIFECYCLE

- Fixes a post-fusion 61-key alias problem that could reintroduce white/black same-key jumps or over-held target notes after source-space sustain repair had already finished.
- Same-onset octave-fold collisions now choose one representative rather than unioning their release tails.
- Genuine in-range C2-C7 notes outrank folded edge-octave aliases when both map onto the same target key.
- A folded alias can no longer manufacture a second press while the native target note is already active.
- When an older folded alias overlaps a later native note, its hold is trimmed before control passes to the native key instead of layering underneath it.
- The rule is target-pitch agnostic and applies to white and black keys; diagnostics report both separately.
- Preserves v0.6.14 acoustic reattack arbitration, v0.6.13 release-safe note-offs, v0.6.12 acoustic pacing, and 88-key exact playback.
- No new setup or model download is required.

Piano Auto Player v0.6.14 - ACOUSTIC REATTACK ARBITER

- Preserves v0.6.13 release-safe note-offs, v0.6.12 acoustic attack pacing, source-space pitch fusion, and target-layout adaptation.
- Adds diagnostics for acoustic reattacks protected, continuity jumps suppressed, and micro-gap jumps suppressed.
